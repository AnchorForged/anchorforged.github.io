1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how AnchorForged (“AnchorForged,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use:

  • Our websites, including anchorforged.com and applyoptics.com (and related pages);
  • ApplyOptics (B2B resume transcription with candidate confirmation; public demo and paid/service paths);
  • Chiefly (our iOS leadership app); and
  • Contact forms, demos, and marketing we operate.

It does not cover third-party sites or apps we do not control (for example Apple App Store account pages, or ads on Meta / TikTok / LinkedIn once you leave our properties).

No invented compliance badges: We do not claim SOC 2, HIPAA, or similar certifications on this page. If we obtain formal attestations later, we will say so explicitly.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Websites (anchorforged.com, applyoptics.com)

  • Contact / inquiry data: name, organization, email, interest area, message content you submit.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser/device type, approximate location derived from IP, pages viewed, referrers, and standard server/CDN logs.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: see Section 6 (including Cloudflare and security/bot tools such as Turnstile where enabled).

3.2 ApplyOptics — demo and service

  • Resume / application materials you or a customer uploads for transcription and structuring (may include names, contact details, work history, education, military/federal career data, and other resume content).
  • Candidate confirmation inputs when a candidate reviews or corrects extracted fields.
  • Account and workspace data for customers (login identifiers, configuration, usage metrics such as confirmed fills).
  • Demo path: the public demo at applyoptics.com/demo is simulated. Treat demo uploads as evaluation data; do not upload secrets you cannot afford to expose.

Process-and-discard intent (where applicable): For certain ApplyOptics flows — especially evaluation/demo and transient processing — we intend to process resume content to produce structured fields and then discard source files and intermediate artifacts when retention is not required for the product feature, customer configuration, security, or law. Production customer workspaces may retain confirmed fields and related records according to the customer’s settings and agreement. We do not use ApplyOptics resume content to build a public people database for sale.

Product honesty: ApplyOptics transcribes and structures; it does not score, rank, or recommend candidates.

3.3 Chiefly (iOS)

  • Account or device identifiers needed to run the app;
  • Content you enter for coaching, journaling, Note Taker, or meeting-capture features;
  • Diagnostic / usage data typical for app reliability;
  • Purchase and subscription status as mediated by Apple (we do not receive your full payment card number from Apple).

Chiefly content can be sensitive to you personally; we process it to provide the feature you request, not to sell profiles.

3.4 Marketing

If you interact with our ads or social campaigns (Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, or similar), those platforms may share aggregated or event-level signals with us (for example campaign performance). If you message us on those platforms, the platform’s rules also apply.

  • Respond to inquiries and provide support;

6. Cookies, Turnstile, and similar technologies

We and our processors may use cookies, local storage, pixels, and security challenges:

  • Essential: load balancing, session continuity, CSRF/bot protection;
  • Cloudflare Turnstile (where enabled): helps distinguish humans from bots without traditional CAPTCHA friction; may involve device/browser signals processed by Cloudflare;
  • Analytics / marketing: only if we enable them; platform pixels (Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn) may set cookies when you visit pages that include those tags.

You can control cookies through your browser. Blocking essential cookies may break forms or logins.

7. Retention

We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes above:

  • Contact inquiries: typically retained to respond and keep a business record, then deleted or minimized when no longer needed;
  • ApplyOptics resumes / intermediates: process-and-discard intent for transient and demo paths; customer production retention follows customer configuration, contracts, and legal holds;
  • Chiefly content: while your account/app data remains active, plus a short period after deletion for backups/security unless law requires longer;
  • Logs: shorter operational windows unless needed for security investigation.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to our size and the sensitivity of data we handle (HTTPS, access controls, fail-closed integration design where credentials are missing). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Report suspected vulnerabilities or incidents to tim@anchorforged.com.
